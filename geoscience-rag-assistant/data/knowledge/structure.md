# Structural geology context

Faults, fractures and lithological contacts can influence fluid pathways and mineral deposition. Distance to a mapped structure may be useful as one feature, but uncertainty in mapping and three-dimensional geometry should be considered.

Combining structural, geophysical, geochemical and geological evidence can be more informative than relying on a single signal. Field validation remains essential because correlations learned from historical data may not transfer to a new region.
