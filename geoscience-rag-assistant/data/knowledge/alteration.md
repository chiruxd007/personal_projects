# Alteration and exploration context

Hydrothermal alteration records chemical and mineralogical changes caused by fluid-rock interaction. Exploration teams may combine mapped alteration minerals, geochemistry, structure and host-rock context to develop a mineral-system hypothesis. An alteration anomaly is not proof of an economic deposit; interpretation depends on scale, mineral assemblage, timing and geological setting.

Remote sensing, core logging and laboratory measurements can provide complementary alteration indicators. Data quality can vary because of weathering, sampling density, instrument differences and incomplete spatial coverage.
